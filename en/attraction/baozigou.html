About

Baozigou is a grape-growing village and scenic area in Hebo Town, Lulong County, surrounded by hills near the 40th parallel — good grape-growing latitude that has earned it the nickname "Turpan by the Bohai Sea." Grapes have been cultivated here for over 300 years, and roughly 1,500 mu of farmland now grow Rose Honey and other varieties such as Sunshine Rose and Kyoho. Since 2002 the village has combined grape farming with rural tourism, offering fruit picking, rock formations, hilltop views, and lakeside fishing. It is a recognized national agri-tourism demonstration site, popular for family fruit-picking trips.

Getting there

By car
Take the Jing-Ha Expressway to the Changlu Road, then head east on Changlu Road for about 1 km. It can be combined with a visit to nearby Changli Grape Valley.

Before you go

  • Grapes ripen progressively from August to October, making this the best window for picking.
  • Entry to the village is free; picking is charged by weight, so bring a shopping bag or cash.
  • It's close to Changli Grape Valley, so the two can be visited on the same day.
  • The village roads are narrow, so drivers should aim to visit at off-peak times.
  • The terrain is hilly, so keep an eye on uneven ground when bringing children or elderly visitors.

Tickets Free to enter; grape picking is charged by weight on site, with the exact rate varying by variety and year. No standard published price was found.

Frequently asked

Is there an admission fee for Baozigou?

The scenic area itself is free to enter; grape picking is charged separately by weight on site.

When is the best time to visit?

August to October, during peak grape season, when several varieties are available for picking.

What else is nearby?

It's adjacent to Changli Grape Valley, so the two can be combined into a single day's itinerary.
