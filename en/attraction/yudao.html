About

Yudao is a large resort combining seawater hot springs, a water park, and beach activities at the middle of the Gold Coast in Beidaihe New District. It has dozens of indoor and outdoor mineral-rich seawater hot spring pools, water park attractions open in summer, and outdoor soaking in winter, making it a year-round destination. A lavender garden blooms from roughly June to August and is a popular photo spot; sand sliding and evening shows are also offered. It suits families and couples looking for a full-day resort-style outing.

Before you go

  • Bring a swimsuit in summer; in winter you can still enjoy the outdoor hot springs, so pack warm clothing to wear between soaks.
  • The lavender is in bloom roughly June to August, the best time for photos.
  • There are several ticket types — comparing prices online in advance usually gets you a better package deal.
  • The park stays open late in summer, so consider arriving in the evening to avoid the daytime heat.
  • The grounds are large, so plan for a full day.

Tickets The basic adult package starts around 135 RMB online (covers park entry, beach, sand/grass sliding, water fort and other basic attractions). A combined hot spring + night show ticket is about 198 RMB. The standalone Feishe'er Seaview Hot Spring ticket is about 188 RMB. Booking online is typically 20-30 RMB cheaper than at the gate, so book a day ahead on a travel platform if possible. There are several ticket types (basic package, hot spring combo, night show combo, etc.), so pick the one that matches your plans.

Hours Summer season (July-August): 8:00 AM-9:30 PM (last entry 9:00 PM), including the night session. Peak season (April-June, September-October): 9:00 AM-5:30 PM, with the hot spring area open until 9:00 PM. Hours are extended to around 6:30 PM during holidays like Spring Festival and National Day — check the day's posted schedule.

Frequently asked

Are the hot springs open in winter?

Yes — outdoor hot spring soaking is the main winter draw, while the water park facilities mainly run in summer.

When does the lavender bloom?

The lavender garden generally flowers from June to August, the best time to see and photograph it.

Does the ticket include the hot springs?

The basic package usually doesn't include the hot springs — you'll need a separate hot spring combo ticket or standalone ticket, so check the ticket details before buying.
