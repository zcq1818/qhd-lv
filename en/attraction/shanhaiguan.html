About

The First Pass Under Heaven (Tianxia Diyi Guan) is the eastern terminus of the Ming-dynasty Great Wall, built in 1381 under general Xu Da. Its gate tower, Zhenyuan Tower, carries a huge horizontal plaque reading "First Pass Under Heaven," each character about one meter square, traditionally attributed to Ming calligrapher Xiao Xian. Visitors can climb the tower for views over the old walled town and the wall itself; the fortified walls, barbican, and nearby Great Wall Museum are well preserved. It is a different ticketed site from the nearby Old Dragon Head (where the wall meets the sea) and from the free-to-enter Shanhaiguan Old Town — don't confuse them.

Getting there

By train & bus
Take a train to Shanhaiguan Station, then walk about 15 minutes to the old town's east gate (First Pass Under Heaven); within the city, buses run to stops near the old town — check current routes via local transit info.
By car
Exit the Beijing-Harbin or Beijing-Shenyang Expressway at Shanhaiguan and follow signs to the old town; about 30-40 minutes from downtown Qinhuangdao.

Before you go

  • The city wall has many steep steps — wear comfortable shoes, and elderly visitors and children should take care.
  • The wall gets very sunny at midday in summer — visit in the early morning or evening and bring sun protection.
  • First Pass Under Heaven, Old Dragon's Head, and Shanhaiguan Old Town are separate attractions with separate tickets — check the exact name before buying to avoid confusing First Pass Under Heaven with Old Dragon's Head (where the Great Wall meets the sea).
  • If you plan to visit Old Dragon's Head or the Great Wall Museum too, compare whether a combo ticket is better value.
  • Peak season and holidays draw large crowds — check the official WeChat account or booking platforms for the day's opening status and reservation requirements.
  • The wall offers open views, great for photographing the "First Pass Under Heaven" plaque and the old town skyline — avoid the harsh midday light.

Tickets Adult ticket around ¥40-50 (price varies by channel and whether it's a combo ticket); a common combo ticket (covering Old Dragon's Head, First Pass Under Heaven, Wangyang Tower, Ying'en Tower, the Bell and Drum Tower, and the shuttle) costs more, around ¥130. Free or discounted admission for children under 6 or 1.2m, seniors over 70, active-duty military, people with disabilities, and blood donors. Whether advance online booking is required could not be confirmed — check the official WeChat account or platforms like Ctrip/Meituan for the current policy.

Hours Peak season (May 1 - October 31): 07:00-18:00; off-season (November 1 - April 30): 07:30-17:00 (sources vary slightly — check the official WeChat account or ticketing platform for the day's posted hours before you go).

Frequently asked

Are First Pass Under Heaven and Shanhaiguan Old Town the same thing?

No. Shanhaiguan Old Town is currently free to enter, while First Pass Under Heaven (the Zhenyuan Tower gate complex) is a separately ticketed 5A attraction within the old town — they aren't interchangeable.

Is First Pass Under Heaven covered by the same ticket as Old Dragon's Head?

No, they're separate paid attractions with different tickets — visiting both usually means buying a combo ticket or two separate tickets.

Who wrote the giant "First Pass Under Heaven" plaque?

It's traditionally attributed to the Ming-dynasty calligrapher Xiao Xian, and hangs on the Zhenyuan Tower — about 600 years old and the site's most iconic symbol.

Are there a lot of steps to climb the city wall?

Yes — the wall stands at a considerable height with fairly steep steps, so wear comfortable shoes and elderly visitors or children should pace themselves.
