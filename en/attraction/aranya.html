About

Aranya is a gated seaside community at Golden Coast in Changli County, Qinhuangdao, known for the "Lonely Library" (Sanlian Seaside Public Library) designed by architect Dong Gong, whose floor-to-ceiling windows face the sea — one of China's first free public libraries by the beach. The community also includes a seaside auditorium, a Dune Art Museum, and a beachfront market, and hosts an annual theatre festival. Access is restricted: non-residents typically need a reservation or to be a paying guest, and the library requires a separate booking via its WeChat account or the Aranya app. It's popular with young creatives, couples, and photographers, especially at sunrise and sunset.

Before you go

  • Photography is not allowed inside the library — step outside if you want to take pictures, and keep your voice down while inside.
  • Book your library visiting slot in advance through the "Seaside Library" WeChat account or the Aranya app — walk-ins are not guaranteed entry.
  • The community is gated; if you're not an owner or overnight guest, the easiest way in is usually to book a homestay/hotel within Aranya or make a qualifying purchase.
  • Sunrise and dusk give the best light, when the white buildings take on golden or pink tones.
  • Light-colored clothing tends to photograph well against the architecture here.
  • Access rules for some venues can change temporarily during special events like the drama festival, so double-check before you go.

Tickets Reservation required. The community itself has no single admission ticket, but some venues or events charge separately or are limited to owners and guests. The Seaside Library is usually free but needs an online reservation for a visiting slot. During special events such as the drama festival, some venues admit only ticket holders or owners, and day-trippers who are not staying overnight may not be able to enter freely — check the official WeChat account in advance for which venues and slots are open that day.

Hours The community is open to visitors all day but is a gated, privately managed area. Seaside Library visiting slots are generally 9:00 AM-5:00 PM, with a daily visitor cap on a first-come, first-served basis — check the "Seaside Library" (Gudu Tushuguan) WeChat account or the Aranya app for the current schedule.

Frequently asked

Can non-owners get in?

Yes, but the community is gated and managed access — you typically need a reservation, an overnight stay at a homestay/hotel inside Aranya, or a minimum amount of spending to enter. Check the current policy before you go.

How do I book the Seaside Library?

Reserve a visiting slot online through the "Seaside Library" WeChat account or the Aranya app. There's usually a daily cap, filled on a first-come, first-served basis.

Can I take photos inside the library?

No photography is allowed inside, and visitors are asked to stay quiet. You can photograph the library's exterior from outside.
