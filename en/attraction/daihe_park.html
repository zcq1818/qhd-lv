About

Daihe Ecological Park stretches along both banks of the Dai River in Beidaihe District, covering about 185 hectares. It blends southern Chinese garden design with the open layout typical of northern gardens, offering wooden boardwalks, a lotus pond, a fishing area, and a ring-shaped path for walking or cycling. Shaded and quiet, it's a favorite spot for locals' morning exercise and a relaxed half-day stop for visitors. Admission is free year-round.

Getting there

By train & bus
Take bus line 45 to the Daihe Ecological Park West Gate (Daihe Shengtaiyuan Ximen) stop; it's about a 13-meter walk from there.
By car
About 6-8 km (15-20 minutes) by car from Beidaihe Station, or about 20 km from Qinhuangdao Station.

Before you go

  • This free community park is a good place to unwind between other stops — no need to set aside a big block of time.
  • Lotus flowers are at their best from roughly June to August, the peak viewing period.
  • Bring a fishing rod if you want to try the designated fishing area.
  • The park is fairly large and good for cycling or walking, so wear comfortable shoes.
  • There's decent shade, but bring sun protection for midday visits in summer.

Tickets No admission fee. A few individual activities inside the park, such as fishing or bike rental, may charge a fee — check the posted price on site (exact figures unverified).

Frequently asked

Is there an admission fee?

No — the park is free to enter year-round.

Is it suitable for children?

Yes. The park is flat and open, with boardwalks and lawns good for a family stroll, though there are few playground facilities — it's mainly about enjoying nature.

When is the best time to visit?

Summer (June-August), when the lotus flowers are in full bloom, is the most scenic time. Spring and autumn are also pleasant for walking or cycling.
