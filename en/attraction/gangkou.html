Qinhuangdao Port Museum

About

Housed in a converted century-old industrial site in Haigang District, this museum traces the history of Qinhuangdao Port, one of modern China's major coal-export ports established around the late Qing dynasty. Exhibits combine old photographs, industrial equipment, and archival material covering the port's growth from a treaty-era harbor to a modern facility. The industrial-style buildings also make it a good spot for photography. Visiting arrangements for individual travelers are currently limited, so checking reservation details in advance is recommended.

Highlights

  • Century-old port history exhibits
  • Industrial heritage buildings
  • Historic dock equipment
  • Creative/cultural district
  • Port culture displays

Getting there

By train & bus
Take bus 8 or 31 within the city to the Nanshan stop, then walk to the museum.
By car
About 3-5 km (roughly a 10-15 minute drive) from Qinhuangdao Station.

Before you go

  • Open hours are split into weekday morning and afternoon sessions, so call ahead to confirm on weekends or closed days.
  • Individual visits may currently be restricted, so call 0335-3093953 or check the 'Leyoueji' mini-program before you go.
  • Admission is free, but bring ID to register.
  • The industrial-style buildings and vintage equipment make for good photos.
  • Exhibits are mostly panels and artifacts, so it will resonate most with visitors interested in port or industrial heritage.
