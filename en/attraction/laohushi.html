About

Tiger Stone Sea Park sits on Beidaihe's central beach, named for a cluster of rocks resembling crouching tigers at the shoreline. Covering about 33,000 square meters, it is free to enter and one of Beidaihe's most popular beaches for swimming, with a gentle slope and fine sand. Paid activities include parasailing, windsurfing, and a children's water park. At low tide, visitors can explore the exposed rocks for shells and small crabs. Sunset here, with the tiger-shaped rocks silhouetted against the sea, is one of Beidaihe's favorite photo spots. It suits swimmers, families, and photographers.

Getting there

By train & bus
Take Bus 34 or Bus 5 and get off at the Tiger Stone stop, right at the entrance.
By car
About 10 minutes by taxi from Beidaihe Station; follow Zhonghaitan Road directly to the park.

Before you go

  • At low tide you can forage among the rocks for shells and small crabs — bring a small bucket and non-slip shoes.
  • 4-6pm is the best window to photograph the sunset silhouette of Tiger Stone.
  • The beach gets crowded during the summer swimming peak, so keep an eye on your belongings and children.
  • Prices for water activities vary by season and operator, so it's worth comparing on site.

Tickets The park itself is free to enter; activities inside such as parasailing, windsurfing, surfboarding, and the children's water play area charge separately. Admission is free and activities are billed per use or per time slot — check the prices posted on site.

Hours Summer hours (April-October): 7:00-22:00; winter hours (November-March): 8:00-18:00. Some sources instead list 6:00-22:00 in summer with last entry at 21:30 — check the notices posted on site.

Frequently asked

Is there an entrance fee for Tiger Stone Sea Park?

The park itself is free, but activities like parasailing and windsurfing are charged separately.

Are Tiger Stone and Pigeon Nest Park the same place?

No — both are on the Beidaihe coast but in different spots. Tiger Stone is known for swimming and sunset views, while Pigeon Nest Park is known for sunrise.

Can I visit Tiger Stone in winter?

Yes, the park stays open in winter (roughly 8:00-18:00), though swimming isn't possible — it's still good for sea views and photos.
