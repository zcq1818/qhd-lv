About

Hongxing Industrial Culture Park is built around the production site of Qinhuangdao Hongxing Steel Group in Changli's circular-economy industrial park, covering about 285,000 square meters as a national 3A-rated industrial tourism site. Visitors can observe the steelmaking process up close, tour a museum tracing the company's nearly 20-year history, and see a carbon-neutrality demonstration zone alongside performances of local Changli intangible-heritage arts. Opened around 2022, the park mainly welcomes pre-booked group visits and is popular for study tours and corporate/Party-building activities.

Before you go

  • Visits are mainly arranged for groups by reservation — individual visitors should call ahead to confirm whether they can enter.
  • The tour focuses on production lines and corporate culture exhibits, mostly indoors, so it works well year-round.
  • A basic understanding of steelmaking processes beforehand can make the visit more rewarding.
  • The park also hosts intangible-heritage performances such as Changli yangge dance, folk songs, chuige music, and shadow puppetry — ask about the current performance schedule.
  • Best suited to group visits such as educational tours or Party-building activities rather than casual individual visits.

Frequently asked

Can individual visitors tour the park?

The park currently operates mainly on group reservations, and whether individuals can enter directly hasn't been confirmed — call ahead to check.

Is there an admission fee?

Available information suggests group tours are free by reservation, but the exact policy and whether individual visitors are charged hasn't been fully verified — ask in advance.

What's the main thing to see there?

Highlights include the Hongxing origin exhibition hall, the steel production line, the carbon-neutrality demonstration zone, and local intangible-heritage performances from the Changli area.
