About

Longyungu Scenic Area sits in Zhanggezhuang Village, Daxinzhai Township, Funing District, its main peak reaching 905 meters. A national 3A attraction, it features a roughly 3,000-meter wooden boardwalk through the canyon, a 530-meter glass skywalk, and North China's first cliff-side rafting course (~2,200 m), alongside waterfalls such as Longtan and Tianchi. In summer the canyon fills with wildflowers from some 28 species, and winter brings striking ice-waterfall scenery, making it a year-round destination for hikers and photographers.

Highlights

  • Wooden boardwalk with a steep "sky ladder" section
  • Longtan and Tianchi waterfalls
  • 530-meter glass skywalk
  • ~2,200-meter cliff rafting course (first in North China)
  • Wildflower fields with about 28 species

Getting there

By car
Take the S52 Cheng-Qin Expressway, exit at Funing North, then head east to Zhanggezhuang village in Daxinzhai township.

Before you go

  • The glass skywalk and the alpine slide/cliffside rafting are paid add-ons on top of the standard admission ticket.
  • The canyon stays cool in summer, making it a popular local spot to escape the heat and play in the water.
  • The wooden "stairway to heaven" section is fairly steep — visitors with limited stamina should pace themselves, and non-slip shoes are recommended.
  • Fall brings colorful foliage and winter brings frozen waterfalls, so the scenery changes noticeably across seasons.
  • Several ticket bundles are available, so compare prices on a booking platform before setting out.

Frequently asked

How much are tickets to Longyungu?

Standard admission is ¥50 per person, with combo packages also available at ¥140, ¥240, and ¥120 with different inclusions — check a booking platform for the current pricing.

Do the glass skywalk and rafting cost extra?

Yes. The 530-meter high-altitude glass skywalk and the roughly 2,200-meter cliffside rafting course are both paid add-ons not included in the base ticket.

Is it open year-round?

Yes, the scenic area is open year-round from 08:00 to 16:30 — summer is good for cooling off in the water, winter for viewing frozen waterfalls, and autumn for fall foliage.
