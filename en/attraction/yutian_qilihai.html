About

Yutian Qilihai Resort sits in the Qilihai area of Beidaihe New District, between Houli and Dahanzhuang villages. Newly rated as a 4A attraction in 2025, it covers about 8,000 mu (roughly 530 hectares) with 5,000 mu of water area. The resort combines large-scale live shows, fishing-culture exhibits, close-up animal encounters (giraffes, capybaras, miniature horses), themed shopping streets, and boutique lodging. Daytime visits focus on animal interaction and water play, while evenings feature a large light-and-water show. It operates seasonally from late April to early October and is best enjoyed as a full-day-into-evening visit.

Getting there

By train & bus
Take the high-speed rail to Beidaihe Station, then a taxi for about 40 minutes. Alternatively, take bus route 808 to the "Dahanzhuang" stop and walk about 10 minutes.
By car
From Beijing, take the Jing-Ha Expressway, about a 3.5-hour drive; from Tianjin, about 3 hours. You can navigate directly to "Yutian Qilihai Resort."
Parking
The resort has a free parking lot (check current on-site signage for details).

Before you go

  • Enter in the afternoon for the water attractions and animal encounters, then stay for the evening show.
  • The night show is the highlight of the resort — it's worth staying until around 9:00 PM to see it.
  • Weekends in peak season get crowded, so book tickets online 1-2 days ahead, or book a lodging package for VIP access.
  • The resort is large (about 8,000 mu, roughly 530 hectares), so expect a lot of walking and wear comfortable shoes.
  • Weekday visits are noticeably less crowded and offer a better experience.

Tickets Adult tickets start at ¥109 (day and night sessions combined; check the booking platform for the exact price on your travel date). Pricing is tiered: adults from ¥109, students/seniors from ¥90, children over 1.2 m from ¥79, with free admission for children under 1.2 m and qualifying seniors. Prices may rise during special events (an International Fireworks Festival ticket was seen priced at ¥238), so confirm the current price through official channels or Ctrip/Meituan before you go. Package deals combining admission with lodging are usually better value than buying tickets separately.

Hours Seasonal operation, roughly April 30 to October 7 each year, daily from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM (last entry and ticket sales at 9:30 PM).

Frequently asked

Is Yutian Qilihai Resort open all year?

No, it operates seasonally, typically from late April to early October, and is closed in winter.

How much does admission cost?

Adult tickets start at ¥109, students/seniors from ¥90, and children from ¥79; check the booking platform for the current price, as it may rise during festivals and special events.

Which is better, the daytime or the night show?

Daytime is great for water play and animal encounters with kids, but the night show's light and water display is the resort's biggest draw — try to stay until evening.
