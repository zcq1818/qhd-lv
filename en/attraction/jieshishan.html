About

Jieshi Mountain, north of Changli County, rises to 695 meters at its main peak, Xiantai Ding. It is one of the few mountains near the Bohai coast that Chinese emperors historically climbed to worship the sea, most famously Cao Cao, who wrote his poem "Gazing at the Sea from Jieshi" here after a campaign in 207 CE. Qin Shi Huang, Emperor Wu of Han and other rulers also visited. The mountain holds old temple ruins and stone inscriptions, and the summit offers views over the Bohai Sea and Changli's Golden Coast. It suits hikers and history enthusiasts; reaching the top takes about two hours on foot.

Before you go

  • The full climb takes about 2 hours and requires reasonable fitness, so wear comfortable hiking shoes.
  • There are few shops on the mountain, so bring your own water and snacks.
  • The summit offers wide views out to the Bohai Sea — pick a clear day for the best visibility.
  • Autumn brings cool weather and better visibility, making it a good time for the climb.

Tickets 20 RMB; some sources list 24 RMB. No clear published discount policy for seniors, children or students was found — check with Ctrip or a similar platform, or at the ticket office, for the latest pricing and combo tickets.

Frequently asked

How much is the entrance fee?

Published sources show around 20 RMB, though some platforms list 24 RMB — check the price posted at the site on the day.

How long does it take to reach the summit?

The full hike to the top takes about 2 hours; the trail has a fair bit of incline, so some fitness is needed.

Are there places to eat or resupply on the mountain?

There are few shops on the mountain, so bring your own water and food.
