About

Shenglan Ocean Park is a marine-themed park in Dapuhe, Changli County, Qinhuangdao, combining animal shows, an underwater viewing tunnel, a 4D theater and family rides. Its dolphin and beluga whale shows are the main draw, with trainers performing close-up interactions with the animals. The park sits next to a beach, making it easy to combine with seaside play in summer. It suits families with children and marine-life enthusiasts; show times are limited, so arriving early for a good seat is recommended.

Before you go

  • Check the day's show schedule as soon as you enter — dolphin and beluga whale shows have limited seating, so get there early to grab a spot.
  • Ticket prices vary a lot by platform, so compare prices on sites like Ctrip or Meituan before booking.
  • In summer you can combine your visit with the park's beach — bring sunscreen and swimwear.
  • Opening hours change with the season, so call ahead or check the booking platform for the current day's hours.

Tickets Adult ticket prices vary by platform — the official list price starts around 88 RMB, with online discount tickets commonly available from about 58 RMB. Free for seniors 70 and over; discounted for seniors 60-69; free for children under 1.2 m tall or under 6 years old. Additional discounts exist for people with disabilities, military personnel, and students — check the day's posted price and the booking platform for details.

Hours Hours vary by season: July 1-August 31, 8:10 AM-6:00 PM; September 1-30, 8:40 AM-5:30 PM. Rides and indoor venues close at 5:00 PM; the beach is open 9:30 AM-5:00 PM (exit closes at 5:20 PM). Hours for the rest of the year were not found — confirm on the official platform before visiting.

Frequently asked

Does the ticket include the animal shows?

Tickets generally include regular dolphin and beluga whale shows — check the posted show times for the day you visit.

Is it open in winter?

The information found only covers hours for July-September; winter opening status and hours were not confirmed, so call ahead or check the booking platform before visiting.

What ages is it suitable for?

The park focuses on marine animal shows and family-friendly rides, suitable for children of all ages, with free entry for those under 1.2 m tall or under 6 years old.
