About

Chateau Kings International Winery, developed by the Tasly Group, covers roughly 45 hectares in the Jieshi Mountain wine region about 18 km from Beidaihe's Golden Coast, and holds national 3A scenic-area status. It combines premium grape cultivation, wine production, cultural displays and tourism, featuring a visitor center, a large themed mural complex (~5,800 sqm), a 360-degree viewing deck, poetry-inscribed walls, a bar/restaurant and a fruit-picking orchard. Its signature Marselan wine has won 152 international awards, and the estate is open to the public for tours and tastings.

Before you go

  • Admission is free, but tasting and other in-park experiences are charged separately — call ahead to ask about package options and prices.
  • The winery's flagship Marselan wine has won numerous awards and makes a good souvenir.
  • The estate covers a large area (about 674 mu, roughly 45 hectares) and is still building out facilities, so some areas may not be fully open yet.
  • The autumn grape harvest (September-October) offers the best experience and can be combined with a tasting for a half-day visit.
  • Combine it with nearby Huaxia Manor and Grape Valley for a full day tour of Changli's wine culture.

Tickets Admission to the grounds is free; tours and tasting experiences inside are charged separately, with tasting packages starting around ¥88. Prices for individual experiences haven't been fully verified through public sources, so check on site or by phone.

Frequently asked

Is there an admission fee for Chateau Kings International Winery?

The grounds are free to enter, but tours and tasting experiences inside require a separate fee, with packages starting around ¥88.

Can you do wine tasting on site?

Yes — the winery offers tasting experiences and packages, headlined by its Marselan wine, which has won 152 international awards.

What are the opening hours?

Reported hours are 09:00-16:00, but confirm with the scenic area's current posted hours or by phone.
