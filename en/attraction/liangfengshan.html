About

Lianfeng Mountain Park, on Lianfeng Road in Beidaihe, is the highest point in the area at about 153 meters, covering roughly 6,068 mu of pine and cypress forest. It is often described as one of China's earliest forest parks. From the hilltop Wanghai Pavilion, visitors get a panoramic view of the Beidaihe coastline. The dense tree cover keeps the park about 5°C cooler than the beach below, making it a popular summer retreat. Other sights include Lotus Rock and Immortal's Cave, plus scattered historic villas. It suits hikers, photographers, and anyone escaping the summer heat, and is known for autumn foliage around October.

Before you go

  • The mountain isn't tall but is densely wooded; wear non-slip athletic shoes, as some steps are steep.
  • It's about 5°C cooler up in the hills than at the foot in summer — a favorite spot for locals to cool off.
  • Autumn foliage is best around mid-to-late October — check the park's updates on peak color.
  • Avoid climbing at midday; the light is also better for photos in the early morning or evening.

Frequently asked

How much is admission to Lianfeng Mountain Park?

About ¥30 on site, with online platforms often offering discounts from around ¥22.9 — booking ahead on Ctrip or similar is worthwhile.

Is Lianfeng Mountain an easy climb?

At about 153m elevation, it's not difficult, but the path has uneven steps, so wear comfortable shoes.

When is the best time to see autumn leaves at Lianfeng Mountain?

Usually mid-to-late October, depending on that year's temperatures and the park's announcements.
