About

Liuhe Valley lies north of Liuhe Beishan Village in Liutianjiazhuang Town, Lulong County, next to the Jidong Anti-Japanese War Memorial Hall. From 1943-1945 it served as the office of the Jidong 12th District Committee, making it a stronghold village of the Jidong anti-Japanese base area — hence its local reputation for "longevity, revolutionary history, and greenery." Surrounded by hills and water, the site has forest cabins and villas, and combines organic farming, fruit picking, fishing, and patriotic-education tourism. It is often visited together with nearby Biixia Manor and other Lulong countryside scenic spots.

Highlights

  • Adjacent to Jidong War Memorial Hall

Getting there

By car
From Lulong County, drive toward Liutianjiazhuang Town; you can navigate to "Liuhe Valley" (Liuhe Xigu) or the "Jidong Anti-Japanese War Memorial Hall," located about 10 km northeast of the town center.

Before you go

  • Combine it with the adjacent Jidong Anti-Japanese War Memorial Hall for a deeper look at the area's revolutionary history.
  • Summer and autumn are ideal for fruit picking and escaping the heat.
  • Admission is free, making it a good family day-trip destination by car.
  • Mountain roads here are narrow, so it's best to travel during daylight.
  • It's close to Liuhe Manor and Zuoyou Valley, so a loop route combining several sites is possible.

Tickets The scenic area itself is free to enter. Fruit picking, fishing, and similar activities inside may be charged separately, but no specific pricing was found.

Frequently asked

Are Liuhe Valley and the Jidong Anti-Japanese War Memorial Hall the same place?

They are adjacent but not the same site. Liuhe Valley is a rural tourism area built around the natural scenery and revolutionary history of Liuhebeishan Village, located right next to the memorial hall.

Is there an admission fee?

The scenic area itself is free; some picking and fishing experiences inside may be charged separately.

What season is best to visit?

Summer and autumn, when the weather is pleasant and the fruit is in season, are the best times to visit.
