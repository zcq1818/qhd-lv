About

Changli Grape Valley lies about 7.2 km northwest of Changli county town, a national 3A-rated agricultural tourism site built around century-old grape trellises. Visitors can walk the shaded vine corridors and pick and taste several fresh-eating grape varieties from July through November, peaking in September. As Changli is known as China's wine town, the valley pairs well with a visit to a nearby winery for a full day of vineyard-themed sightseeing.

Highlights

  • Wine tasting nearby

Getting there

By train & bus
Take transport to Changli County town first, then a local bus or taxi on to Xishanchang village; there's no direct high-speed rail or long-distance bus service.
By car
From Qinhuangdao or Beidaihe railway station, drive along the coastal expressway toward Changli, then head northwest about 7 km after reaching the county town; the full trip takes roughly 40-60 minutes.

Before you go

  • Peak picking season is August-September, though different grape varieties are ready for picking anywhere from July to November.
  • Admission to the valley is free, but picking grapes inside the orchards is usually charged by weight — ask about pricing before you start.
  • Combine it with nearby wineries such as Huaxia Manor and Chateau Kings International Winery for a full wine-country day trip.
  • Summer and autumn sun in Changli can be strong, so bring sun protection and wear light shoes suited to walking between the fields.
  • There are many independent growers on site, so it's worth comparing a few before you buy; weekends in peak season can get crowded.

Tickets Admission to the scenic area itself is free; entering individual picking orchards to harvest grapes is charged separately, either by weight or by each orchard's own rules, and prices vary by vendor — confirm with the growers on site.

Frequently asked

How much does it cost to enter Grape Valley?

The scenic area itself is free to enter at any time, but picking grapes inside the orchards is typically charged separately by weight.

When is the best time to go grape picking?

The picking season generally runs from July to November, with most varieties at their peak — and the best overall experience — in August and September.

Are there wineries nearby to visit as well?

Changli is known as China's wine-producing hometown, and nearby wineries like Huaxia Manor and Chateau Kings International Winery can easily be visited on the same day.
