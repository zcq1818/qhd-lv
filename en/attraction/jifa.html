About

Jifa Agricultural Dream Kingdom, on Haibei Road in Beidaihe, evolved from an earlier eco-agriculture park and began upgrading toward a higher scenic-area standard in 2018. It combines agricultural technology displays, farming-culture exhibits, children's rides, and educational tours for school groups. Large greenhouses grow a range of tropical and subtropical plants and fruit, alongside exhibits on modern farming techniques. A petting area lets children interact with small animals, and there are several kid-friendly rides. Because the greenhouses stay warm year-round, the park works well as an indoor option on rainy or cold days, and especially suits families and school groups.

Before you go

  • The greenhouses can feel hot and humid, so wear breathable clothing and drink water regularly.
  • Discounted tickets are often available on Ctrip or Meituan in advance, cheaper than buying at the gate.
  • A half-day visit works well and can be combined with nearby attractions.
  • If bringing children, leave time for the petting-animal area and kids' play equipment.

Tickets Around ¥50 (some sources instead list ¥30; prices may shift with season or events). Prices vary considerably between sources (¥30-50) and may fluctuate with season or evening events, so check the current price and any discounts on Ctrip or Meituan before buying.

Hours Peak season (May-December): 8:30-17:00, with last entry at 16:00. Some sources say hours extend to 21:30 during certain periods — check the day's notice posted at the site.

Frequently asked

Can I visit Jifa Agricultural Dream Kingdom on a rainy day?

Yes — the greenhouse areas are indoors, so a rainy day doesn't stop the visit; it's a good rainy-day option.

How much is admission?

Prices vary by channel, roughly ¥30-50 — check the current price on Ctrip or Meituan before buying.

What age is it suitable for?

Suitable for children of all ages, especially school-age kids who enjoy plants, animals, and educational content.
