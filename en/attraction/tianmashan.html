About

Tianma ("Heavenly Horse") Mountain sits about 10 km north of Funing District, Qinhuangdao, rising to roughly 300 m. It is named for a summit boulder resembling a galloping horse. Ming-dynasty general Qi Jiguang, who defended this stretch of the Great Wall, left several rock inscriptions here, now a provincial cultural relic. The mountain features stacked boulders, caves, and whimsically shaped rocks such as "Monkey Watching the Sea." It's an easy half-day hike combining natural scenery with Ming border-defense history.

Highlights

  • Qi Jiguang's cliff inscriptions
  • Horse-shaped summit rock formation
  • "Monkey Watching the Sea" rock
  • Natural caves
  • Summit views over the Yanshan range and Bohai Sea

Getting there

By car
Follow National Highway 102 to a point about 9-10 km north of Funing District's urban area. It's about 45 km from downtown Qinhuangdao, roughly a 1-hour drive.

Before you go

  • Call ahead or check a booking platform to confirm the current ticket price and opening hours before setting out.
  • The main highlight is the Qi Jiguang cliff carvings, so watch the rock faces along the trail as you climb.
  • The mountain is low (about 300 m) and the round trip is about 4 km, manageable for most fitness levels.
  • The trail is mostly stone steps and loose gravel, so wear non-slip shoes.
  • Autumn offers the clearest views, ideal for taking in the scenery from the summit.

Tickets Online sources disagree and no single confirmed price could be found; figures range widely, from free admission to around ¥35, or ¥60 for adults with a 50% student discount (about ¥30). No recent official announcement was found, so call the site directly or check real-time pricing on Ctrip or Meituan before you go.

Hours Roughly 8:00 AM-5:00 PM, with last entry at 4:00 PM; check the day's posted hours at the site, as these can vary.

Frequently asked

How much does admission to Tianma Mountain cost?

Online information is inconsistent, so it's best to call the site or check a booking platform for the day's actual price before you go.

What's the top attraction at Tianma Mountain?

The Qi Jiguang cliff carvings near and along the way to the summit, a provincial-level protected cultural relic.

Is the hike difficult?

The peak is about 300 m in elevation with a roughly 4 km round trip, mostly on stone steps and loose gravel — manageable for ordinary visitors.
