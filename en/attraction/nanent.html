About

Nandaihe International Amusement Center, in the Nandaihe resort area of Funing, Qinhuangdao, is one of the region's older and larger amusement parks, built around roller coasters, a Ferris wheel and a water park. In 2025 the park upgraded several attractions, including a sea-view Ferris wheel, a dome-screen theater, a themed roller coaster, a challenge ride and a space-shuttle-style ride. With a large footprint and a mix of thrill rides and family-friendly attractions, it suits both younger visitors seeking excitement and families with children. Lines can be long during peak summer weekends, so an early, weekday visit is recommended.

Before you go

  • The park is large, so arrive early and plan your route to fit in the major rides.
  • Summer weekends draw big crowds and long queues — visiting on a weekday is less hectic.
  • Booking online ahead of time is usually cheaper than paying at the gate, so compare prices on ticketing platforms first.
  • Hours change significantly by season and holiday, so confirm the day's schedule on a booking platform or official channel before you go.

Tickets Prices shift with the season and ride combinations; group-buy deals can start as low as 29 RMB, so check the booking platform's current price. Free entry for children under 6 or under 1.2 m tall, seniors 70 and over, active-duty military, people with disabilities, firefighters/rescue workers, and holders of the "Yingcai A Card." Half-price for children 6-18, full-time undergraduate or lower-level students, seniors 60-69, and people with disabilities. Confirm the exact ticket type and whether it covers all rides before buying.

Hours 2025 posted hours: May 1-June 30, 8:30 AM-5:30 PM; July 1-August 25, 8:00 AM-6:00 PM; August 26-31, 8:30 AM-6:00 PM; September 1-October 7, 9:00 AM-5:30 PM; extended to 8:00 AM-6:00 PM during holidays such as Labor Day, Dragon Boat Festival, Mid-Autumn Festival and National Day. Frequently asked

How much is admission to Nandaihe International Amusement Center?

Prices fluctuate a lot by season and sales channel, with group-buy deals as low as 29 RMB — check the booking platform's current listing, as there's no single official fixed price.

Is it open in winter?

The information found only covers May-October; winter opening status was not confirmed, so check ahead before visiting.

Is it suitable for young children?

The park has both thrill rides and gentler family-friendly attractions, so you can pick rides suited to your child's age and height.
