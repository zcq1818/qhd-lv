About

Laojunding Scenic Area sits in Shimenzhai Township, Haigang District, Qinhuangdao, its main peak rising 493.7 meters and ringed by steep hills on three sides, opening south toward the Bohai Sea. Blending Taoist heritage with the local geology and river landscape, it is best known for summer whitewater and canyon rafting routes of roughly 2.5 km and 3.5 km, and for a winter ice-and-snow carnival with skiing, skating and curling. It draws families, young travelers and outdoor enthusiasts across both seasons.

Highlights

  • High-altitude ring rafting (~2.5 km)
  • Longxu River canyon rafting (~3.5 km)
  • Winter ice-and-snow carnival
  • Riverside fireside camp

Getting there

By train & bus
Bus route 820 from the Haiyang Road bus station in Qinhuangdao goes directly to the scenic area, running roughly 7:20-9:00 AM outbound and 2:20-4:00 PM for the return trip.
By car
About 30-40 km from downtown Qinhuangdao, along winding mountain roads to Fangzhuang village in Shimenzhai township; the drive takes roughly 1 hour.

Before you go

  • Rafting is the main draw — if visiting in summer, bring a change of clothes and a waterproof bag for electronics.
  • Bus route 820 runs infrequently, so a private car or chartered ride offers more flexibility.
  • The winter ice and snow carnival includes skiing, skating, and curling, which are charged separately and may require booking equipment in advance.
  • Prices vary significantly between ticket types (alpine rafting, valley rafting, sightseeing, season pass), so compare prices on a booking platform before you go.
  • Summer weekends and the peak holiday season can get crowded — consider visiting on off-peak days or booking ahead.

Tickets There's no admission fee just for hiking the mountain; rafting and similar activities are ticketed separately — roughly ¥90 for the alpine rafting course and ¥130 for the valley rafting course, with lower group-buy starting prices on third-party platforms. Tickets are sold by category (rafting, sightseeing, snow-park, etc.); platforms like Qunar have shown canyon-rafting promo tickets from ¥9.9 and alpine-rafting deals from ¥80, with actual prices fluctuating by season and platform promotions — check the official channel or a booking platform for the current price before buying.

Hours Open 08:00-17:00; seasonal activities like rafting follow the scenic area's current-season announcements, and the winter snow programs typically run from December through February.

Frequently asked

How much does it cost to visit Laojunding?

There's no charge just for hiking the mountain. The main expense is the rafting activities — about ¥90 for alpine rafting and ¥130 for valley rafting — with lower promotional prices often available on third-party platforms.

How do I get there by bus?

Take bus route 820 from the Haiyang Road bus station in Qinhuangdao directly to the scenic area. It departs roughly 7:20-9:00 AM and returns 2:20-4:00 PM; since service is infrequent, plan ahead.

What can you do at Laojunding in winter?

The scenic area hosts an ice and snow carnival with skiing, skating, and curling — a sharp contrast to the summer rafting.
