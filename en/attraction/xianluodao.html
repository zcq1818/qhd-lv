About

Xianluo Island is a landmark island scenic area in the Nandaihe resort district of Funing, Qinhuangdao. Visitors reach the island via a roughly 1,038-meter cross-sea cable car (about 15 minutes each way), which offers panoramic views of the coastline. The island has a seaside viewing promenade and several paid attractions, including sea bungee jumping, boat rides, a rotating car ride, a carousel and a pirate ship. The cable car crossing is the main highlight and makes this a popular stop in Nandaihe, suited to families, couples and photo-focused visitors; those afraid of heights should be cautious on the cable car.

Before you go

  • The cable car across the sea is the main highlight and the primary way to reach the island — those afraid of heights should think twice before riding.
  • Sea bungee jumping, boat rides and similar attractions on the island are all extra, so budget accordingly.
  • Pick a clear day for the best sea views and photos.
  • The round-trip cable car ride takes about 15 minutes, so allow extra time for queuing.

Tickets Admission, which includes a round-trip cable car, is about 80 RMB. Sea bungee jumping costs an extra 240 RMB per person, boat rides an extra 80 RMB per person, and rides such as the spinning taxi, deluxe carousel and pirate ship are about 35 RMB per person. The ticket usually covers the round-trip cable car across the sea, while bungee jumping, boat rides and other attractions on the island are all charged separately. No clear published discount policy for seniors or children was found — check before buying.

Hours Published hours cover July 10-December 31: 9:00 AM-7:00 PM. Hours for the rest of the year were not found — check with the ticketing platform or the site before visiting.

Frequently asked

What does the Xianluo Island ticket include?

It usually includes the round-trip cable car across the sea; bungee jumping, boat rides and other rides are bought separately on-site.

Is the cable car safe? Can people afraid of heights ride it?

It's a standard sightseeing cable car across the sea and is generally considered safe, but since the whole ride is suspended above open water, those afraid of heights should decide for themselves whether to ride.

Is it open in winter?

The published hours found only cover July-December; opening status and hours for winter (January-June) were not confirmed, so check ahead.
