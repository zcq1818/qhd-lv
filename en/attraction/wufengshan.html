About

Wufeng (Five Peaks) Mountain lies north of Changli County, about 10 km from Jieshi Mountain. Its five distinctively shaped peaks, the highest around 507 m, form a screen-like ridge long listed among the "Ten Scenes of Ancient Jieshi." It is also historically significant as a site where the early Chinese Marxist Li Dazhao took refuge and wrote key essays. A statue of Li Dazhao, inscribed rocks, and the Ming-dynasty Hanwengong Shrine remain on the mountain, making it a mix of hiking and revolutionary-history tourism.

Highlights

  • Li Dazhao historic site
  • Ming-dynasty Hanwengong Shrine
  • Five screen-like peaks
  • Feilai Peak (507m) hike
  • Li Dazhao statue and rock inscriptions

Getting there

By train & bus
From Changli Railway Station, minivans run directly to the scenic area.
By car
From downtown Qinhuangdao, take the Jing-Ha Expressway to the coastal expressway, exit at Changli/Golden Coast, then follow the Changli-Huanghai connector road onto Wufengshan Road. The drive takes about 1 hour from the city center.

Before you go

  • Ticket prices are low, making this a good value half-day trip.
  • The trails are mostly hiking paths, so wear non-slip athletic shoes.
  • Combine it with nearby Jieshi Mountain or Changli Golden Coast for a fuller itinerary.
  • The site has strong revolutionary-history content, making it a good history lesson for kids.
  • The forest cover is dense, so watch for mosquitoes in summer.

Tickets Around ¥13 (some online sources list ¥10 instead, so figures are inconsistent across platforms). No information was found on combo tickets or online booking requirements; check the current price posted at the site or on a booking platform before you go.

Hours Open all day, with no published difference between peak and off-peak hours. It's best to visit during daylight and head back down before sunset.

Frequently asked

Is Wufeng Mountain suitable for elderly visitors or children?

The mountain isn't very high, but the route is mostly on foot, so elderly visitors and children with limited stamina should pace themselves and stick to the gentler sections.

What Li Dazhao-related sites are on the mountain?

There is a statue of Li Dazhao, several inscribed rock carvings by notable figures, and the site near the Ming-dynasty Han Wengong Temple where he once took refuge to read and write.

How much is admission?

Online sources put the price at around ¥10-13; check the price posted on site for the exact figure.
