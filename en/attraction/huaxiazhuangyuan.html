About

Huaxia Manor, part of COFCO's Great Wall Winery brand, was founded in 1988 on a roughly 5.6 million-square-meter estate north of Changli town. As a national 4A scenic area and one of China's first industrial-tourism demonstration sites, it houses Asia's largest underground wine cellar alongside vineyards, an automated bottling line and a wine research institute. Visitors can tour the cellar, see grape cultivation demonstration plots, and join guided tastings, making it a good stop for wine enthusiasts and families interested in China's red-wine heritage.

Highlights

Before you go

  • Reported ticket prices vary online (roughly ¥30-80), so confirm the current price through an official channel or booking platform before you set out.
  • You can add a wine-tasting package on site to sample several dry red wines.
  • The autumn grape harvest (September-October) is the best time to visit, and it pairs well with a stop at nearby Grape Valley.
  • The underground wine cellar is noticeably cool, so bring a light jacket.
  • As a designated industrial tourism site, the grounds are fairly large — budget at least 2-3 hours for a visit.

Tickets Tickets run roughly ¥60-80 depending on the season, though the site's own historical data lists ¥30 — a discrepancy from more recent figures — so check the official or third-party booking price on the day of your visit. Reports indicate free admission for children under 1.2 m and seniors over 70, and some sources mention a half-price discount for Qinhuangdao residents with local ID (possibly an outdated policy). Prices and discounts change between peak and off-peak seasons, so confirm the current price and whether wine tasting is included via a platform like Ctrip or by phone before buying.

Hours Generally open 08:30-17:30 on regular days and 08:00-18:00 on holidays. Other sources give different seasonal hours: roughly 08:40-16:30 from April 1 to October 15, and roughly 09:10-16:30 from October 16 to January 31 and February 2 to March 31 — check the hours posted on site.

Frequently asked

How much are tickets to Huaxia Manor?

Recent reports put ticket prices at roughly ¥60-80 depending on the season, though some sources list ¥30 — figures vary, so confirm the current price through an official channel or booking platform before you go.

Can you do wine tasting there?

Yes. The estate offers wine tasting, usually as a separately purchased package or bundled with a combo ticket.

Is the underground wine cellar worth seeing?

Yes — it's Huaxia Manor's signature feature, billed as the largest underground wine cellar in Asia, and one of the main highlights of a visit.
