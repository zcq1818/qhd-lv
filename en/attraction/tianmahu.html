About

Tianma Lake Scenic Area sits about 9 km north of Funing District, Qinhuangdao, centered on a reservoir with a horse-shaped landmark sculpture on its shore. It historically offered boating, fishing and simple water rides for family outings. As of recent reports (2025), the site has been temporarily closed with no confirmed reopening date, so visitors should verify its status before planning a trip.

Highlights

  • Boating on Tianma Lake
  • Lakeside fishing
  • Horse statue landmark
  • Hillside lake views

Getting there

By train & bus
No confirmed direct bus route; a taxi from the Funing District bus station is recommended if you go, though check whether local routes have been temporarily altered given the site's closed status.
By car
From downtown Qinhuangdao, take the Jingshen Expressway to National Highway 102 into Funing District, then head north about 9 km; the full trip takes roughly 40 minutes. The area is near the Jingshen Expressway and National Highway 102.

Before you go

  • This scenic area is currently closed — call ahead or check online to confirm whether it has reopened before making the trip.
  • If it has reopened, historical accounts suggest the site is small and can be seen in 1-2 hours.
  • It can be combined with nearby Tianma Mountain and Laojunding, if you're building an itinerary around the area.
  • There is no official explanation for the closure or an expected reopening date — don't rely on secondhand information found online.
  • Historically, activities here centered on family leisure and fishing rather than major attractions, so keep expectations modest even if it reopens.

Tickets IMPORTANT: Tianma Lake is currently closed/suspended, and ticket information will only be confirmed if it reopens. The historical listed price was about ¥10, with free admission for children under 1.2 m and active-duty military personnel, but this reflects the most recent historical data available, not a current price. The site is not currently operating — call the Funing District tourism authority to check its latest status before planning a visit.

Hours CLOSED: As of 2025, multiple sources report the scenic area is suspended and not operating. Historical opening hours were 08:00-17:00, but there is no official announcement of a reopening date.

Frequently asked

Is Tianma Lake Scenic Area still open?

According to reports circulating since 2025, the scenic area is currently suspended, with no official announcement of when it might reopen. Call the local tourism authority or the site itself before visiting.

How much do tickets cost?

The current price cannot be confirmed. Historical records show a listed price of about ¥10, with free admission for children under 1.2 m and active-duty military personnel — for reference only.

Are there similar attractions nearby?

Funing District and the surrounding area have alternatives such as Tianma Mountain, Laojunding, and Longyungu, all nature-based scenic spots.
