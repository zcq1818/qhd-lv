About

Liuhe Manor is a winery-resort in Fengjiashan Village, Liutianjiazhuang Town, Lulong County, built by a local wine company. It combines wine-grape vineyards, a winemaking facility, and manor-style grounds with leisure lodging in wooden cabins, making it a representative "wine plus tourism" destination near Qinhuangdao. Visitors can tour the vineyards and winery, learn about the winemaking process, and relax on the grounds. It suits wine enthusiasts, business groups, and couples looking for a quiet countryside getaway.

Getting there

By car
From downtown Qinhuangdao, drive via Bixia Manor and Mujing Town toward Liuhe Manor; the trip takes about 1-1.5 hours (check real-time navigation for the current route).

Before you go

  • Autumn (September-October), during grape harvest, is the best time to visit for picking and tasting.
  • Call ahead or check online to confirm on-site experiences like tastings and lodging.
  • It can be combined with the nearby Liuhe Valley and Zuoyou Valley for a one-day route.
  • Corporate groups and couples may want to consider staying in the manor's log cabins.
  • Plan your driving route in advance, as GPS signal can be weak in parts of this mountainous area.

Tickets The manor itself is free to enter; wine tasting, lodging, and picking experiences are usually charged separately, with no standard published pricing found online — it's best to contact the manor in advance to confirm. A "9D amusement park" mentioned in some online sources could not be independently verified in this research, so treat that claim with caution.

Frequently asked

Is there an admission fee for Liuhe Manor?

Touring the grounds is free; add-on experiences like wine tasting and lodging are usually charged separately.

Can you taste or buy wine on site?

As a winery-affiliated estate, it typically offers tasting and wine sales, though this depends on what's available on the day — it's best to inquire in advance.

When is the best time to go?

Autumn, during the grape harvest and winemaking season, offers the best atmosphere.
