About

Yansai Lake sits in Shanhaiguan District, Qinhuangdao, about 3.5 km northwest of the old Shanhaiguan fortress town. Formed in 1974 when a dam was built across the Shi River, it became the first man-made lake at the eastern end of the Great Wall; the river once served as the fortress's natural defensive barrier and was the site of historical battles from the Sui-Tang era through the 1933 Great Wall resistance against Japan. Known as the "Guilin of the North" for its green hills and clear water, the lake offers boat cruises past unusual rock formations and a bird park with over 100 species. It is especially popular in autumn for colorful foliage.

Getting there

By train & bus

By car
About a 20-minute drive or taxi ride from Shanhaiguan Old Town (roughly 3.5 km).

Before you go

  • Confirm whether the boat ticket needs to be purchased separately before boarding.
  • Autumn (around September-October) has the most vivid foliage and is the best time for photos and scenery.
  • The lake area is fairly large, so allow 2-3 hours for the visit.
  • The bird sanctuary (Niaoyulin) is a good stop for families with young children.
  • Admission and hours change with the season, so check official channels or a travel platform for the current information before you go.
  • It pairs well with Shanhaiguan Old Town on the same day, since the drive is short.

Frequently asked

Does the Yansai Lake ticket include the boat ride?

Sources disagree — some list admission at ¥90, others from ¥50 or a group-buy price of ¥45, and we couldn't confirm a consistent answer on whether the boat ride is included. Check with the official channels or a travel platform before buying.

How long should I plan for Yansai Lake?

Generally 2-3 hours, including a boat ride on the lake and exploring the shoreline scenery.

Can I visit Yansai Lake and Shanhaiguan Old Town on the same day?

Yes, they're about a 20-minute drive apart, making them easy to combine in one day.
