About

Qinhuangdao Wildlife Zoo, on Binhai Avenue in Beidaihe, is one of northern China's larger wildlife parks, covering roughly 5,000 mu. It is split into a drive-through predator zone, where visitors view tigers, lions, and bears from their cars, and a walk-through zone where guests can hand-feed giraffes, alpacas, and other gentle animals, plus a bird enclosure and herbivore areas. It's open year-round, with animals generally more active in the morning and in warmer months. The zoo is a popular half-day to full-day family outing in the Beidaihe area.

Getting there

By train & bus
Take Bus 34 and get off at the Wildlife Zoo stop, right at the entrance.
By car
About 15-20 minutes by taxi from Beidaihe Station, via the coastal road (Binhai Dadao).

Before you go

  • Keep windows fully closed while driving through the predator area, and never get out of the car or feed the animals.
  • Visit in the morning when the animals are more active for better viewing.
  • In the tame-animal area you can buy feed in advance to feed giraffes, alpacas, and similar animals — a hit with kids.
  • The park is large with little shade in summer, so bring sun protection and plenty of water.

Tickets Official full-price ticket around ¥100; online platforms often show discounted prices of ¥72-80. Free admission with ID for children under 6 or 1.2m, seniors over 70, people with disabilities, and active-duty military. Booking online is generally cheaper than buying on site — book ahead on Ctrip or Meituan. Admission usually includes the safari shuttle/drive-through predator area.

Hours Summer (April 1 - October 31): 8:30-17:30; winter (November 1 - March 31): 8:30-16:30. Open year-round, including holidays, on the same summer schedule.

Frequently asked

Is it safe to drive through the predator area?

Yes — the area has safety barriers, but you must keep all windows closed and stay in the vehicle, following staff instructions.

Does the ticket include the drive through the predator area?

Admission usually includes the shuttle or drive-through fee for the predator area — check the details on the ticket page.

What age qualifies for free admission?

The common threshold is children under 6 or under 1.2m tall, with discounts for seniors over 70 and others — check current policy before buying.
